OFF THE CV MAKER 2.0Make your own.

Your CV burns onto a CD, an iMac boots, and a podcast about your work plays in your own voice. Your own Claude builds all of it, on your machine.

Say this

Use Wispr to tell your Claude:

Make your own sameep.ai using my GitHub profile.

Hold your Wispr Flow key in Claude Code and say it. Or type it.

First time? Give your Claude the skill once, so it knows what “sameep.ai” means:

curl -fsSL https://sameep.ai/skill/off-the-cv/install.sh | sh
Read it first

Not on Claude Code? Paste sameep.ai/llms.txt into any agent along with the sentence.

How it works
  1. It asks once. Your GitHub name, then anything else you want in: a CV (PDF or LinkedIn export), a photo, 30 seconds of your voice. All optional. One message, so it never stalls halfway.
  2. It builds. Pulls your projects, burns your CV into the hero, writes the episode in your voice, draws a pixel-art you, clones your voice on your own Mac, then makes the lip-sync and subtitles.
  3. You check, then it ships. A local preview first. A privacy scan. Then GitHub Pages or Cloudflare, only when you say yes.

What it won’t do
  • Make anything up. Every line traces to your CV, a README or something you told it.
  • Use a face or a voice that isn’t yours. The hosts are fictional, with preset voices.
  • Publish your email, phone, address or keys. The CV that burns has no contact details.
  • Send your visitors’ data to me. My analytics are switched off in your copy.
  • Publish anything before you’ve seen it and said yes.

What Switchboard is

A free Mac app that lends your own Claude (and your local voice engine) to web pages, one consent at a time. With it, this page can read your work, write your episode and render your voice, and you pay me nothing because I pay nothing.
