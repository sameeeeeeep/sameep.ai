What Switchboard is

A free Mac app that lends your own Claude (and your local voice engine) to web pages, one consent at a time. With it, this page can read your work, write your episode and render your voice, and you pay me nothing because I pay nothing.

Get Switchboard

Or do it all from Claude Code

Install the skill, then type /off-the-cv. It reads your repos, writes the episode, makes the sprites, clones your voice locally and deploys to GitHub Pages.

mkdir -p ~/.claude/skills/off-the-cv
curl -fsSL https://sameep.ai/skill/off-the-cv/SKILL.md \
  -o ~/.claude/skills/off-the-cv/SKILL.md

You can still use the steps below without it: copy the prompt into any Claude, paste the JSON back, make the sprites here, and record a voice sample to clone later.
