How it works

Talk it out. Paste it in.

  1. Speak

    Brain dump

    Hold fn and talk: the story, the numbers, the link, who to thank. Rough is fine. Your own Claude turns it into a post for each platform, written the way that platform reads, with your facts and links kept.

  2. Plan

    The calendar

    Week and month views. Every post moves from idea to draft to your OK, then scheduled and posted. Nothing is queued until you approve it, and an edit sends it back to you. Drag it to another day, rewrite it for another platform, add an image or a video.

  3. Post

    The reminder

    At post time a small card appears. The copy is already on your clipboard and the image is selected in Finder. Copy the next post in the thread or the first comment with a click, then mark it posted.

What's real

What works now, and what's coming

Works today

  • Brain dump to X thread, LinkedIn post, Instagram caption and slides
  • Rewrite one post for the other platforms
  • Week and month calendar, saved as plain files on your Mac
  • Best time: three slots per post, in your audience's timezones
  • Your OK on every post: read it in full, approve, and only then is it scheduled
  • Reminder at post time, copy on your clipboard
  • Videos from Wispr Video, my voice video editor mod

Coming

  • Publishing for you, through the X, LinkedIn and Instagram APIs, or Buffer and Typefully. It's built as a switch that's off, so you can already see exactly what it would send.
  • Best times from your own numbers, not rules of thumb

Reddit and Hacker News stay manual on purpose.
