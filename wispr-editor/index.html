How it works

Three steps, all by voice

Record, say the edit, export. Video keeps your footage on your Mac the whole way.

1

Record like you talk

The teleprompter lights up each word as you say it and moves on only when you finish the line. It never runs on a timer. Press space or → to jump ahead.

Camera and screen are separate tracks. Record them together, or add the screen pass later at the playhead.

Video follows your voice through the recording’s own mic. You can also hold your Wispr key and read: the dictated words move the prompt.

2

Say the edit

  • “Cut the pauses and the ums.”
  • “Put the challenge page over the second line.”
  • “Show the site full while I talk about it.”
  • “Make the captions bigger, one word at a time.”

Type it, dictate it with Wispr, or say it to Claude Code. The timeline updates live.

3

Export everywhere

9:16 for Reels, 4:5 for the feed, and 16:9 for YouTube. Video burns in word-timed captions with your keywords highlighted, using ffmpeg on your Mac.

Speech-to-text runs on-device with whisper.cpp. Your footage never leaves your Mac.

For Claude

Claude edits it, you watch

The project is a plain JSON edit list. Claude reads it (clips, script lines, and every word with its time) and sends ops back through a small MCP server. Video watches the file, so you see each change land.

video_edit({ ops: [
  { "op": "cut_silences", "min_gap": 0.45 },
  { "op": "overlay", "src": "challenge-page", "start": 5.49, "end": 20.66,
    "box": { "x": 0.08, "y": 0.12, "w": 0.84, "h": 0.46 }, "fit": "contain" },
  { "op": "captions", "size": 0.055, "words": 3 },
  { "op": "keywords", "add": ["Wispr", "2,000"] }
]})
Install

Try it yourself

One command. Nothing is written to your settings: Video runs for this session only.

claude --plugin-url https://sameep.ai/wispr-editor/wispr-video.zip

Then tell Claude what the video is about, like /video a 45-second launch for my app. Claude writes the script, opens the teleprompter, and edits your take when you’re done.

Needs macOS, Chrome, Python 3, Xcode Command Line Tools, ffmpeg, and whisper-cpp (brew install ffmpeg whisper-cpp). The first run downloads a 140 MB speech model. Voice-follow has been tested in Chrome with a simulated mic so far. Projects live in ~/WisprVideo.
