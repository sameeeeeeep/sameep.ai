Nailinit × Khushi Kapoor · an actual sleepover

The Secret Slumber Party

Pyjamas, a ball pit and a nail bar. About thirty-five spots, and you had to apply, by chatting with Khushi on a little pink retro screen.

My part

The party was a team effort, but the whole online side was mine: the application, the site, the arcade and the emails. For the night I built the live show on the big screen, plus a little run-of-show tool so the decor people, the DJ and the drivers each knew when to turn up.

Why a chat, not a form

The first version was a normal form, and it felt like paperwork. So I rebuilt it as a chat. Each question is a tiny reply, and it feels like being let in.

I tracked every question on its own, so I knew exactly where people gave up. After you applied, an arcade with high scores kept people coming back to beat each other.
