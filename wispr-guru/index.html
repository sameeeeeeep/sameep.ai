Wispr Guru a prototype by Sameep for Wispr

A second cursor that shows you how.

Ask out loud how to do something in any app. Guru points at the button, tells you the step, and waits. Say “done” and it moves on. Try it on the document below.

Launch notes

Open questions: pricing for teams, and whether the guide should speak its steps out loud.

How it works

  1. Ask. Hold your Wispr key and say what you want: “where’s the setting for…”, “how do I export this?”
  2. See. Claude looks at your screen and finds the control through the Mac’s accessibility tree, so the pointer follows the window if you move it.
  3. Do. A second cursor points, and a small card says the step. Say “done”, “go back” or “wait, where?”. Guru waits for you.

On your Mac

Guru is a Claude Code mod. Claude gets a guide tool and a /guru command, so it can walk you through anything on your Mac. When a thread finishes while you’re elsewhere, Guru meows (or plays a short metal riff, your call) and shows a small card you can answer by voice.

claude --plugin-url https://sameep.ai/wispr-guru/wispr-guru.zip

Needs an Apple silicon Mac on macOS 13+, a recent Claude Code, and Wispr Flow for voice. Typing works too.
