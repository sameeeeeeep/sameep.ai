How it works

Ask, see, done.

Guru waits for you at every step, and moves on when you click the thing it points at.

  1. Ask

    Hold your Wispr key and say what you want: “where’s the setting for…”, “how do I export this?”

    Hold fn and speak
  2. See

    Claude looks at your screen and finds the control through the Mac’s accessibility tree, so the pointer follows the window if you move it.

  3. Do

    A second cursor points, and a small card says the step. Click it and Guru moves on. Lost? Click Where?

On your Mac

Guru lives in Claude Code

Claude gets a guide tool and a /guru command, so it can walk you through anything on your Mac. When a thread finishes while you’re elsewhere, Guru meows (or plays a short metal riff, your call) and shows a small card you can answer by voice.

claude --plugin-url https://sameep.ai/wispr-guru/wispr-guru.zip

Needs an Apple silicon Mac on macOS 13+, a recent Claude Code, and Wispr Flow for voice. Typing works too.
