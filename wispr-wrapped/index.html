Get your real Wrapped: give this to your Claude.

Use Wispr Flow and Claude Code on a Mac? Run this in Terminal. Claude reads your own Flow history on your Mac and builds your Wrapped there, with every card as a PNG. Nothing gets uploaded.

claude --plugin-url https://sameep.ai/wispr-wrapped/wispr-wrapped.zip "/wispr-wrapped"

That starts Claude with the Wrapped plugin and runs /wispr-wrapped.
