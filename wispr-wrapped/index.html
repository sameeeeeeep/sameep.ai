Make yours

Your real Wrapped, made on your Mac.

Wrapped reads your own Flow history and builds every card on your Mac, each as a PNG. Nothing gets uploaded. You need Wispr Flow and Claude Code. Run this in Terminal:

claude --plugin-url https://sameep.ai/wispr-wrapped/wispr-wrapped.zip "/wispr-wrapped"

That starts Claude with the Wrapped plugin and runs /wispr-wrapped.
