Wispr for Builders made for Wispr by Sameep · unofficial

Pack 1 · 7 mods for Claude Code

Say it. Your Mac does it.

Wispr is for typing, and typing is for everything. These mods take what you dictate with Wispr Flow and act on it: find the file, convert it, fix the site, park the task. I used these to build sameep.ai.
