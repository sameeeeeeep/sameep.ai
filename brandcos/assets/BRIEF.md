# brandcos landing: the brief

## The idea

A brand is three things: its story, its people, its processes. Everything else (the decks, the sheets, the WhatsApp groups) only holds those three together, and badly. The founder is what really holds them together. She is the router, the memory and the reminder, and she is the reason nothing moves while she is asleep.

A chief of staff that never sleeps changes who carries the company. brandcos listens to what the company already says in meetings, WhatsApp and email. It turns that into work and assigns it. It does the work it can do. It brings people in only to decide, review or do the physical thing. The founder stops being the place where everything gets stuck and goes back to being the person who decides.

The page should not explain this. It should show it happening, over one day at a real brand.

### The founder's day, before

- 7:10 am. 46 unread WhatsApp messages across 9 groups. Two are about the same shade.
- 10 am. The vendor call ends. Nobody wrote down the MOQ she agreed to.
- 1 pm. The warehouse says 300 units are on the shelf. Shopify says 112.
- 4 pm. The creator's shoot is tomorrow and no one has sent the brief.
- 11 pm. "Ma'am, the Blinkit listing is showing the old price." She fixes it herself.
- 1 am. Invoice from the filler, GST number wrong. She will remember it on Thursday.

### The founder's day, after

- 7:10 am. One page: what happened overnight, what it already handled, and three things that need her.
- 10 am. The vendor call becomes four tasks, each owned, each due.
- 1 pm. The stock mismatch is a card in the ops group, with the count, the cause and a suggested fix. Two people vote and it is done.
- 4 pm. The shoot brief went out yesterday, drafted by the agent and approved with one tap.
- 11 pm. The listing price was caught at 10:52 and corrected in a draft. It waits for her approval in the morning.
- 1 am. Nothing. She is asleep, and the company is not.

### The single image that carries the page

**The morning brief on the founder's phone, resting on a stack of shade swatch cards and a single liquid lipstick, in the first warm light at 7am.** One hand is just at the edge of frame, reaching for the cup. The company ran all night and this is the handover. Every other scene on the page is one of the threads that ends up in that brief.

Hero line: **"Your brand ran all night. Here is what it did."**
Sub: brandcos is the chief of staff for brands and agencies. It finds the work in your meetings, WhatsApp and email, gets it done, and brings people in only to decide.

## Art direction (one look for every image)

- **Genre:** editorial still photography of a modern Indian D2C beauty brand's working world, Mumbai. Documentary, not staged advertising. Real surfaces with real wear: kraft cartons, steel racks, terrazzo, teak, cotton.
- **Light:** natural window light, soft and directional, from the left at roughly 45 degrees. Warm morning or late-afternoon colour temperature (about 4300K). Night scenes use one practical source only (phone screen, a desk lamp). No studio strobes, no rim light, no glow, no haze.
- **Palette:** warm neutrals (bone, sand, kraft, oat, terracotta) with deep brown-black shadows. The product colour is the only saturated note: a muted rose-brown "nude" and one deep berry. No neon. The UI's lime (#c8f250) appears only on the screens in frame, never in the set.
- **Lens:** 50mm full-frame look, f/2.8, eye-level or slightly above. Shallow but readable depth of field. Overhead 90-degree shots only for the flat-lay scenes. No fisheye, no tilt-shift, no drone.
- **Finish:** clean digital capture, grain-free, crisp, neutral contrast, no film emulation, no vignette, no lens flare, no colour grading tricks.
- **People:** no real people's likenesses. Faces turned away, cropped out of frame, or out of focus behind the subject. Hands, forearms, shoulders and backs only. Indian skin tones, everyday clothes, a kurta, a hoodie, a warehouse vest.
- **Product:** a generic liquid lipstick, a frosted glass tube with a matte black cap and doe-foot applicator, no logo or text on the pack. Shade cards and labels blank or handwritten with shade codes only (S-04, S-07).
- **Exclusions on every prompt:** no logos, no brand names, no readable real-company text, no faces, no celebrity likeness, no watermark, no emoji, no extra fingers, no text in the image.
- **Screens in frame:** phone or laptop screens are left as a clean dark rectangle in the photo. The real product screenshot is composited into it in the build, so the UI stays sharp and true.
- **Aspect:** 3:2 landscape for desktop, with a safe 4:5 crop centred on the subject for mobile.

## Scenes

### 1. The morning brief (hero)

- **Copy:** "I used to wake up to 46 unread chats. Now I wake up to one page."
- **Image prompt:** Editorial photograph, overhead three-quarter angle, a smartphone lying on a teak table beside a small stack of blank shade swatch cards and one frosted glass liquid lipstick tube with a matte black cap, a ceramic cup of chai, first warm morning window light from the left casting long soft shadows, a woman's hand in a cotton kurta sleeve entering from the bottom edge of frame reaching for the cup, face out of frame, warm bone and sand palette with a muted rose-brown accent, 50mm, f/2.8, grain-free, crisp, the phone screen a clean dark rectangle, no logos, no text, no faces.
- **Product screen:** Today brief (narrative daily brief, KPI tiles, "3 things need you").

### 2. Vendor and shade trials

- **Copy:** "We tested eleven shades in two weeks. I never had to ask what the filler said."
- **Image prompt:** Editorial photograph of a manufacturer's sample table in a small Indian cosmetics lab, a row of eleven unlabelled frosted glass liquid lipstick tubes in rose, nude and berry tones, swatches painted on white card in neat stripes with handwritten shade codes, a gloved hand holding one applicator mid-swatch, a laptop at the edge of frame with a dark screen, soft window light from the left, warm neutral palette, 50mm, f/2.8, shallow depth of field, grain-free, no logos, no faces, no readable text other than shade codes.

### 3. The warehouse shelf and the kitting table

### 8. A candidate interview

- **Copy:** "We hired a warehouse lead in nine days. The agent ran the funnel; I met the last two people."

### Close

- **Copy:** "A brand is its story, its people and its processes. brandcos keeps all three moving, and calls you only when it needs you."
- **Image:** reuse scene 1, a later moment: the phone face-down, the cup empty, the lipstick tube uncapped.
- **Product screen:** none. The CTA sits on the image.

## Notes for the build

- Brand in all copy: un:fltrd, a Mumbai D2C makeup brand. The product details of the liquid lipstick are placeholders: shade codes S-04 and S-07, drop date "in 9 days".
- Copy is in the founder's voice: first person, plain, no product jargon, at most two sentences per scene.
- Each screen is a real product screenshot, captured from brandcos/app at 1440 and 390, composited into the dark screen area or set beside the photo. Do not draw fake UI in CSS.
- Do not edit brandcos/app/. Another workflow owns it.

## Revision 2 (12 Nov): reality on the screens, one device per frame

- **Screens are the real app, rendered from a fixture.** brandcos/app's own page modules render a seeded un:fltrd workspace (fixture JSON served in place of the API, clock fixed at 7:10 am IST), captured at 2x. One screen per beat, matching the line: Today (Good morning, 3 need you), the NPD board with S-04 moving Plan to In progress, the S-04 stock card at 0, 1 and 2 votes, the MRP card in Revision with Approve plan, the creator brief with every step done, the Move-the-shoot card at 0 and 2 votes, the GSTIN invoice held, the Warehouse lead finalists, and the evening brief for the close. No sample-data pills, no agents-live dot, brand spelled un:fltrd.
- **The UI lives on the device in the photo.** Phone scenes (7:10 am, 10:52 pm, 11:04 pm, the close) map the capture onto the photographed screen with a corner-pinned matrix3d, then push the camera in until it reads. There is never a dead device beside a floating copy.
- **Each beat has its own frame.** 10 am: swatch close-up with the board as a side strip. 1 pm: split, shelf against the two numbers and the vote. 4 pm: unpinned, the brief card over the photo's edge. 11:04 pm: the phone glowing on a dark desk, the vote card dropping in from the top. 1 am: flat lay beside the held invoice. Nine days: a funnel, 140 to 1. Captions sit on a local scrim, no boxes.
- **Captions are narration**, present tense with the time, not quotes. One plain line says the day is illustrative and the numbers are sample data.
- **The ledger became a recap rail** after the scenes: a thumbnail per beat at its time, the old way struck through, the new way under it, each a link back.
- **Plates regenerated:** 4 (10:52 pm, a phone held in a dark bedroom), 6 and 7 (props removed, no lipstick, no chai), 8 (the interview at the kitting table on the warehouse floor). The hero product appears only in the hero, lab, warehouse, shoot and close.
