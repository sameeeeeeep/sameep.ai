Your first two weeks

On every plan. By day 10, your team is making decisions on cards instead of in group chats.

  1. Day 1
    Kick-off call

    We map your story, people and processes, and pick the areas you want agents in.

  2. Days 2 to 3
    Your tools connect

    Shopify, Meta, Razorpay, Shiprocket, marketplaces, WhatsApp and email, each into the company hub.

  3. Day 4
    Your first morning brief

    Yesterday's numbers, what's running low and what needs you, written from your own data.

  4. Day 5
    Your team logs in

    One join link. Everyone lands in the groups for their role. Sensitive roles wait for your approval.

  5. Days 6 to 7
    Boards from your real work

    Launches, restocks, campaigns and hiring, each moving through plan, final, in progress and done.

  6. Day 8
    Proactive intake turns on

    It reads your meetings, WhatsApp and email, finds the work, and assigns an owner and a checker.

  7. Day 9
    First decisions on cards

    Options, a recommendation, everyone's votes, one tap. Nothing goes out without a person.

  8. Day 10
    Training for each team

    Each team learns its own group, board and approvals, on a call or right in the chat.

  9. Days 11 to 14
    Review and tune

    We look at what got stuck, tighten the rules, and run your first weekly review together.

Questions

Where does my data live, and who can see it?

Each brand gets its own deployment and database. Your team sees only what their role allows; customer details show only in the groups you choose, such as support. I never share your data with another brand.

Can the agents do something I didn't approve?

No. Agents draft. Anything that changes something or leaves the company, like a site edit, an ad budget, a purchase order or a reply to a customer, comes to a person as a card first. Every approval and check is logged with who and when.

Which tools does it connect to?

Shopify, Meta Ads, Google Ads, Razorpay, Shiprocket, Blinkit and Zepto, marketplaces, WhatsApp, Gmail, Drive, Calendar and sheets. Most connect in under a minute during set-up.

What if a tool I use isn't supported?

We start from an export or a sheet so you're not blocked, and I build the connector in your hands-on hours. On Budgeted and Balanced, a new connector can be added as custom development.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any month. The set-up fee isn't refundable once your company is set up, and you can export your data when you leave.

We're an agency with many brands. How does it work?

Each brand gets its own workspace and set-up. Your team gets one login across them, and an agency group in each brand you run. Talk to me about pricing for three or more brands.

What does an hour with you cover?

A call or working session with any of your teams, or work I do for you inside your groups: setting up a workflow, a dashboard, a connector or training a new person.
