Often · the startup that didn’t work

Often & Trippy

A travel startup I co-founded. Trippy was the AI we built for travel hacking: what to book, when, and rebooking you if the price dropped.

My part

Product, tech and the customers. Looking after those trips taught us more than anything we planned.

What I learned

In travel, whoever controls the supply, the hotels and the flights, wins. And people barely use a travel app, so it never becomes a habit, and word of mouth has to spread extremely fast.

We ran out of money. Hard lesson. But everything I’ve built since has the sharing built into the thing itself.
