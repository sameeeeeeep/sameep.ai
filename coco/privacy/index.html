Privacy policy

Coco, by sameep.ai. Last updated 16 November 2026.

What Coco is

Coco is a workspace for consumer brands and the people who run them. It reads the sources a workspace connects and helps its team and agents act on them. This policy covers the Coco app and the data it handles.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, used to sign you in.
  • Gmail, if you connect it: with read-only access (the gmail.readonly scope), Coco reads message metadata (sender, recipients, subject, date), the snippet, and the plain-text body, trimmed. Attachments are not downloaded.
  • Google Calendar, if you connect it: read-only access to event titles, times and attendees.
  • Other sources your workspace connects: for example a store, ads accounts or WhatsApp messages sent to your workspace's Coco number. Only what that source shares with Coco.
  • Work you do in Coco: tasks, approvals, messages and settings.

How we use it

Only to provide Coco to your workspace:

  • showing your sources in your workspace;
  • letting your workspace's agents sort incoming items, find threads waiting on a reply and suggest tasks and decisions;
  • keeping a record of who approved what.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use Google user data to train general AI models. People at sameep.ai do not read your mail, except with your permission to fix a problem you report, for security, or where the law requires it.

Google user data

Coco's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it is kept

Coco runs on Cloudflare. Each workspace's data is kept apart from every other workspace's. Sign-ins and keys for your sources are stored encrypted, and are never shown back in the app or passed to agents.

To read and sort items, Coco's agents send the item text to an AI model provider (Anthropic). Only the items being worked on are sent, for that workspace only.

Sharing

We share data only with the services that run Coco (Cloudflare for hosting, Anthropic for the AI model), and only to provide Coco. We never share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Keeping and removing it

  • You can disconnect a source at any time from your workspace's Sources page. Coco then stops reading it.
  • You can also remove Coco's access in your Google Account under Security, Third-party access.
  • To delete your account or a workspace and everything Coco holds for it, email hello@sameep.ai. We delete it within 30 days.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@sameep.ai.
