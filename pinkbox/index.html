Nykaa · big-sale product · 2019–21

The Pink Box

A wishlist that turns into your cart the second the sale goes live. A feature of Nykaa’s biggest sales.

What it was

Nykaa’s big sales are the ones the whole app gears up for: the Hot Pink Sale, Pink Friday, the Birthday Sale.

The Pink Box was one of them. Before the sale you fill it like a wishlist, and the moment the sale goes live, it all drops into your cart.

What I took away

The buzz is a product, not a post. If there’s something to do every day before the sale, people come back on their own, and the sale day kind of takes care of itself.

  • +9%landing page conversion
  • +30%sale conversion
  • +35%revenue from ads on the site
