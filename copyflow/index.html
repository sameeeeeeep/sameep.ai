Copyflow · a Switchboard wrapp

Show a workflow once. It runs itself.

Record a two-step demo, like fetching some data and adding a row, and your own Claude turns it into a routine you confirm once. Switchboard runs it on a schedule.

  1. Show it once: do the task while Copyflow watches
  2. Your Claude generalises it into a routine with settings you confirm
  3. Switchboard runs it on schedule; pause or run it any time

How it stays safe

It runs on your own Claude through Switchboard. The operator holds no key and never sees your data.
